Queen's Domain gear stats

Every weapon and accessory in the game, read from the game files (14 Nov 2026 build), including the 38 Witch's Grimoire items (purple G tags). Click a column heading to sort, hover a name for its in-game description. Numbers are damage you deal before enemy resistances.

How the numbers work
  • Your attack power = 10 + 0.75 per player level + 0.75 per Meat upgrade + flat "+damage" bonuses, then multiplied by damage-multiplier gear (Brutal Amulet ×1.25 and so on). Every weapon multiplies this.
  • Light hit = attack power × the weapon's light-attack power. Melee weapons work like King's Field: each swing empties a power meter that refills over the weapon's meter time, and a swing deals its power in proportion to how full the meter was. So damage per second = light hit ÷ meter time, whether you wait for a full meter or swing early. Three weapons (Explorer's Cudgel, Plasma Spear, Plasma Sword) keep 25% power on an empty meter, so spamming them does a bit better.
  • Charged hit: holding a light attack to charge it multiplies it by 1.333.
  • Alt attack is the weapon's second attack (right button). Some need ability charges, built up by hitting or over time.
  • Weapon level: each level adds +3% power. At the cap the bonus jumps to the cap itself, so a level-10 weapon hits ×1.30 and a level-25 weapon ×1.75. Every Grimoire weapon caps at 25; most others at 10. Upgraded (the upgrade machine) replaces this with a fixed value and the level bonus no longer applies; "—" means the weapon cannot be upgraded.
  • Guns fire at their rate per minute and use energy cells; throwing weapons recharge over their cooldown. Their "per second" is hit × shots per second, or hit ÷ cooldown.
  • Damage types matter: an enemy weak to a type takes ×1.25, one that resists it ×0.5. "Absolute" damage is not resisted.
  • Not modelled: combo and multi-hit animations, projectiles from alt attacks, status effects and the individual swing speed of each moveset.
